About

I'm KubixDev, an indie developer working in Unity. Most of what I do revolves around shaders: I like experimenting with how surfaces, light and materials look on screen, and turning those experiments into assets.

Besides that, I make games, and I usually go for a realistic style. Most of my games are still in development, so they usually aren't released on itch.io yet. I publish my assets there and post videos on YouTube. Everything I make, I make independently.
